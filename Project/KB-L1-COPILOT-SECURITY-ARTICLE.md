# Knowledge Base Article - L1 Self-Service
## If You Saw Unexpected Information in Copilot

**Version:** v1.0  
**Date:** 07/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

---

## What's Happening

If you opened Copilot (the AI assistant on your computer) and it showed you a document or information you didn't expect to see—especially something marked confidential—please read this.

**Your data is safe.** This is not your fault.

---

## What You Should Do

**Step 1: Don't panic.** Your information is secure and being protected.

**Step 2: Keep your computer on and don't restart it.** The security team needs to check your device exactly as it is now.

**Step 3: Don't delete or clear anything** from your computer, browser, or files. Leave everything as it is.

**Step 4: Call the Help Desk immediately.**

---

## What to Tell the Help Desk

When you call, mention:
- "I saw unexpected information in Copilot"
- The document name or client name (if you remember)
- What time you first saw it
- That your computer hasn't been restarted since

**Help Desk Number:** [Insert number]

---

## Why This Matters

Our security team is investigating. Your device helps them understand what happened and fix it. By keeping it unchanged, you're helping us protect everyone.

**Your data was never at risk. You did the right thing by reporting it.**

---

*For any concerns, contact the Help Desk—they're here to help.*
