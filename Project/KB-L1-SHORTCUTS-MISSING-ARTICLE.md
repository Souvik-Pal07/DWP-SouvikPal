# Knowledge Base Article - L1 Self-Service
## My Desktop Shortcuts Are Missing - Should I Worry?

**Version:** v1.0  
**Date:** 07/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

---

## What's Happening

If your desktop shortcuts (like Recycle Bin, OneDrive, or company apps) appeared to disappear when you logged in this morning, you're experiencing a known issue on Floor 6. We deployed a new backup feature Friday—your computer is moving your desktop files to cloud storage for safety. During this move, shortcuts seem to vanish, then they come back.

**Your data is safe. Nothing was deleted. This is temporary.**

---

## What You Should Do

**Step 1:** Wait for your login to complete (about 30 minutes). Your files are being backed up.

**Step 2:** After login finishes, look at your desktop. Your shortcuts will be there.

**Step 3:** If shortcuts don't reappear after 45 minutes, restart your computer.

**Step 4:** If shortcuts are still missing after restart, call Help Desk.

---

## What to Tell Help Desk

When you call, mention:
- "My desktop shortcuts appeared to be missing"
- The time it happened
- Whether they came back on their own
- That you're on Floor 6
