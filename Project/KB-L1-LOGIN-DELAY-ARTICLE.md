# Knowledge Base Article - L1 Self-Service
## My Login is Taking Forever - What's Happening?

**Version:** v1.0  
**Date:** 07/08/2026  
**Status:** Draft

---

## What's Happening

If your login took 30+ minutes instead of the normal 10 seconds, you're experiencing a known issue on Floor 6. We deployed a new backup feature Friday—your computer downloaded it and is using it on your first login. This is working as designed, but it makes login slower one time.

**Your data is safe. Your computer is fine. This only happens once.**

---

## What You Should Do

**Step 1:** Wait for your login to complete (about 30 minutes). Your computer is backing up your files.

**Step 2:** Once logged in, restart your computer. Your next login will be back to normal speed.

**Step 3:** If login takes longer than 45 minutes, restart and call Help Desk.

---

## What to Tell the Help Desk

When you call, mention:
- "My login took 30+ minutes this morning"
- The time it happened
- That you're on Floor 6

**Help Desk Number:** [Insert number]

---

*Everything is working normally. You did nothing wrong. Questions? Call Help Desk.*
