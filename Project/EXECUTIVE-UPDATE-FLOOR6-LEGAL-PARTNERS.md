# Executive Update: Floor 6 Incident Review

**Audience:** Legal partners  
**Prepared by:** Service Delivery Manager  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  

## What happened
Floor 6 users reported two service problems after the recent desktop environment change. First, sign-in became much slower than normal. Second, desktop shortcuts appeared to be missing during sign-in. The evidence reviewed so far points to a cloud backup setting applied to Floor 6 that moved desktop content while users were logging in. A separate report was also raised about sensitive information appearing in Copilot. That matter is being handled separately as a security concern.

## Impact observed
The slow sign-in delayed the start of work for Floor 6 users and created avoidable disruption. The missing-shortcuts issue caused understandable concern that files had been lost, although the evidence indicates the files were moved rather than deleted. The Copilot report raised a confidentiality concern and is being reviewed under the security process.

## What is being done
The service team has completed the initial review and identified the most likely cause of the two service issues. The corrective change path has been prepared and is ready for controlled action. The security report has been escalated to the appropriate review group, and the supporting evidence has been preserved.

## Current status
The login issue and the shortcut issue are under controlled technical review and remediation planning. The Copilot matter remains under security investigation.

## What remains under investigation
We still need to complete final confirmation of the service fix and determine the exact cause and scope of the Copilot-related report.

## Assurance statement
We are treating both the service issues and the security report with appropriate urgency and care. Based on the evidence reviewed so far, there is no indication that user data was deleted. The security matter is being handled separately so that legal and privacy obligations are considered before any broader conclusion is made.
