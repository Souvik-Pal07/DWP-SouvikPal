# DWP FinBridge Win 11 Migration — End-User Feedback Theme Analysis

**Source:** 50 post-migration comments from FinBridge staff  
**Analyst:** DWP Post-Migration Review  
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Theme Analysis

---

### Theme 1: Account Lockout / AVD Access Failure

**Description:** Users locked out of their accounts or unable to sign in to Azure Virtual Desktop, in some cases for extended periods with no resolution.

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Count** | 7 (comments: 1, 11, 16, 21, 29, 37, 45) |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Representative Quotes:**
> "Cannot log in to AVD at all since this morning. Tried 3 times. Urgent." *(#1)*

> "Account locked again, this is the third time this week." *(#29)*

---
