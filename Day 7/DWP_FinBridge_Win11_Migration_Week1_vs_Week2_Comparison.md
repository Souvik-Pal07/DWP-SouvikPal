## ⚠️ New Theme in Week 2 — Not Present in Week 1

---

### NEW: Excel Crashing on Large Files

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Week 2 count** | 9 (comments: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37) |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

Excel is crashing repeatedly when opening files over approximately 10MB. Unsaved work is being lost. The issue is concentrated in the finance team who work with large budget spreadsheets and is described as the single biggest remaining productivity problem.

This did not appear in week 1 at all — it is either a post-migration software compatibility issue introduced by the Win 11 update or a new Intune configuration conflict. Given it has generated 9 comments in week 2 alone, it is now the most urgent open issue.

> "Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time." *(W2 #13)*  
> "Excel crashing has become a real productivity problem for finance team." *(W2 #20)*
