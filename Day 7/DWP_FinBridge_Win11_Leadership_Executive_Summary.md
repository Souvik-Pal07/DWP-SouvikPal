# Digital Workplace Migration — Executive Summary
## FinBridge Win 11 Project Status

**To:** Leadership  
**Date:** 2026-08-12  
**Migration Phase:** Week 2 Post-Deployment

---

## Overall Picture: Migration on Track

The Windows 11 migration was successful for the vast majority of staff. In the first week, we identified several connectivity and access issues — account lockouts, VPN drops, missing files, and slow login times. The IT team resolved all of these within seven days. Feedback shows marked improvement in week two, with users explicitly thanking us for the fast turnaround and 18% of staff now reporting improved performance and speed compared to their previous setup. Login times are back to normal, file access is restored, and remote work connectivity is stable. Most staff are working without friction.

## Remaining Issues and Immediate Actions

Two issues are open and require urgent attention. **First**, the floor 3 printer mapping has now been unresolved for three weeks despite promises of resolution. The entire floor 3 team has resorted to walking to another floor to print, affecting both productivity and our client meeting deadlines. This requires an owner and a confirmed fix date today. **Second**, a new issue emerged this week: Excel is crashing when the finance team opens budget spreadsheets over 10MB, causing unsaved work loss and blocking month-end reporting. This appears to be a software compatibility issue introduced by the Windows 11 update. Both issues have been escalated to the technical teams. The printer issue is with facilities/print services, and Excel is with the applications team. We expect diagnosis and a path forward on both by end of week. Affected staff have been given workarounds and priority support contact channels.
