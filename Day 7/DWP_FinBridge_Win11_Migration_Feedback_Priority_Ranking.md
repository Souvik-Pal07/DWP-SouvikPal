# DWP FinBridge Win 11 Migration — Feedback Priority Ranking

**Source:** 50 post-migration comments from FinBridge staff  
**Analyst:** DWP Post-Migration Review  
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## Top 3 Actions for Today

Ranking methodology: severity is the primary weight — all Blocker themes outrank all Friction and Minor themes regardless of volume. Within the same severity tier, volume breaks the tie. A theme with persistent, multi-day evidence or client-facing impact is elevated over a same-count peer.

---

### #1 — Account Lockout / AVD Access Failure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Count** | 7 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #1:**  
Highest volume among Blockers and the most acute individual impact — users are completely unable to work, some waited over 2 hours with no callback. The issue is recurring (multiple users locked out multiple times in the same week), which means it is not a one-off. Total loss of access to the machine or AVD session has zero workaround.

**Manager summary:**  
> Seven staff members have been repeatedly locked out of their accounts or AVD since migration, some waiting hours for resolution, and this needs same-day escalation to the identity / Active Directory team.

---

### #2 — Floor 3 Printer Mapping Failure

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Count** | 6 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #2:**  
Second highest Blocker volume and — critically — the issue has persisted for at least three consecutive days with no fix delivered despite IT commitments. The entire floor 3 team is affected (not just individuals), staff are now walking to floor 2 to print client documents, and a client meeting deadline was explicitly flagged. Duration and team-wide scope elevate this above a lower-count Blocker.

**Manager summary:**  
> The floor 3 printer has not mapped on login for at least three days, the whole team is unable to print client documents, and an IT-promised fix has not materialised — this needs an owner and an ETA today.

---

### #3 — VPN Instability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Count** | 4 comments |
| **Severity** | Blocker |

**Why it ranks #3 over OneDrive (also 4 comments, also Blocker):**  
Both themes are tied on volume and severity. VPN is ranked higher because the impact is visible to external clients in real time — VPN drops are happening mid-call and described as "unacceptable for client work." OneDrive is a critical issue (see full theme list below) and would be the very next action, but VPN disruption carries immediate reputational risk that cannot wait.

**Manager summary:**  
> VPN is dropping multiple times per hour during client calls post-migration, four staff have raised it this week, and it is actively damaging the organisation's professional image with external clients.

### Notes on themes not in top 3

- **OneDrive Files Missing (4, Blocker):** Immediately follows VPN as the next priority. File loss carries a data integrity risk and two users flagged hard deadlines (end-of-day report, client meeting). Should be actioned same day if capacity allows.
- **Shared Drive Inaccessible (3, Blocker):** Finance-specific; blocking month-end reporting. Escalate to storage/permissions team.
- **Slow Login (3, Friction):** Consistent all week — likely a Group Policy or profile load issue worth a targeted investigation, but users can still work.
- **Minor UI / Cosmetic (9, Minor):** Highest raw count in the dataset but zero work impact — no action needed beyond a self-service FAQ.
- **Positive (9, Positive):** Confirms migration was successful for a significant portion of staff; useful context for the manager communication.
