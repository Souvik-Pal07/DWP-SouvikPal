# FinBridge Win 11 Migration — Self-Service Guidance for Common Issues

**Audience:** All FinBridge staff  
**Date:** 2026-08-12

---

## 1. Locked Out of Your Account or Can't Sign In

**Try this first:**

1. Wait 15 minutes without attempting to sign in again — repeated failed attempts will extend the lockout.
2. Visit the self-service password reset page at **aka.ms/sspr**, enter your work email, and follow the steps to unlock your account yourself.

**Contact the Service Desk if:**  
You cannot access the reset page, do not have a registered phone or email for verification, or you are still locked out after resetting. When you call or raise a ticket, tell them: **your full name, your device name (shown on the sticker on your laptop), and the exact error message on screen.**

---

## 2. Printer on Floor 3 Not Showing Up

**Try this first:**

1. Sign out of your computer and sign back in — this refreshes your printer connections and often brings the printer back automatically.
2. If it still does not appear, click **Start → Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners** and check whether the floor 3 printer is listed.

**Contact the Service Desk if:**  
The printer is still missing after signing back in, or it shows as "offline." Raise a ticket and include: **your name, your room or desk location, and whether the printer disappeared after a recent update or was never there from the start.**

---

## 3. VPN Keeps Disconnecting

**Try this first:**

1. When the VPN drops, close the VPN app fully, wait 30 seconds, then reopen it and reconnect.
2. If you are on Wi-Fi, try moving closer to the router or switching to a wired connection if one is available at your desk.

**Contact the Service Desk if:**  
The VPN disconnects more than twice in a single day, or reconnecting does not help. When you contact them, note down: **the time each drop happened, whether you were on Wi-Fi or wired, and what you were doing when it dropped (e.g. on a call, downloading a file).**
