## What Is This Article For?

Use this guide if you log in to your work computer and your shared work folder — the one that normally appears as the **S drive** or **Finance** when you open your folders — is no longer showing up.

**Your files are completely safe.** Nothing has been deleted. The files are still stored securely on the office system. This issue only affects the connection between your computer and that folder — the files themselves are untouched.

---

## Before You Call — Try These Steps First

These are safe to do yourself and fix the problem in many cases.

**Step 1 — Wait five minutes and check again.**
After you log in, your computer sometimes takes a few extra minutes to connect to everything it needs. Wait five minutes, then open any folder on your computer by clicking the **yellow folder icon** on your taskbar, and check whether the shared folder has appeared on the left-hand side.

**Step 2 — Sign out and sign back in.**
- Click the **Start** button (bottom-left of your screen).
- Click your **name or profile picture** at the top or bottom of the Start menu.
- Select **Sign out**.
- Wait 30 seconds, then sign back in with your usual username and password.
- Open any folder on your computer and check whether the shared folder has appeared on the left-hand side.

**Step 3 — Restart your computer.**
If signing out did not help:
- Click **Start** → click the **Power** icon → select **Restart** (not Shut down).
- Allow the computer up to **five minutes** to fully start up — this is normal.
- Sign back in and check whether the shared folder has appeared on the left-hand side.

**Step 4 — If the folder is still missing after a restart, contact the Service Desk.**
Do not try anything else. The team will take it from here.

---

## Contact the Service Desk If…

- The shared folder is still missing after restarting.
- You see an error message when trying to open the folder.
- Other people in your team have the same problem.
- You urgently need access to a file in the missing folder.

---

## Details to Have Ready When You Call

Having these details will help the team fix the issue faster — you do not need to understand what they mean, just read them out:

- [ ] Your **full name** and **team or department**
- [ ] Your **computer's name** — click the **Start** button, then click the **cog icon** (Settings), then click **System**, then click **About**. Your computer's name will appear next to the words **Device name** on screen. Read that name out to the team.
- [ ] The **floor and room** you are working in
- [ ] **What time** the folder went missing
- [ ] What the **folder was called or how it appeared** — for example, the letter S, or the word Finance
- [ ] Whether **colleagues nearby** have the same issue
- [ ] The **exact wording** of any error message you see (take a photo on your phone if helpful)

---

*Not sure about any step? Skip it and call the Service Desk straight away. The team is here to help and there is no wrong answer.*
