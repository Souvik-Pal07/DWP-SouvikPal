# Help Guide: I Can't Log In or My Computer Shows Errors When Turning On

## What Is This Article For?

Use this article if your work computer is showing an error when you try to log in, or it is taking much longer than usual to reach your main screen and your normal programmes are not working.

**Your files and data are completely safe.** This issue is related to how your computer joins the office system when it turns on — nothing on your computer has been deleted or changed.

---

## Before You Call the Service Desk — Try These Steps First

These steps are safe to do yourself and fix the problem in many cases.

**Step 1 — Wait two minutes and try again.**
Sometimes the issue clears on its own once the systems have had a moment to catch up. Wait two minutes, then try logging in again normally.

**Step 2 — Sign out and sign back in.**
If you are already on your main screen but your programmes are not working:
- Click the **Start** button (bottom-left of your screen).
- Click your **name or profile picture** at the top or bottom of the Start menu.
- Select **Sign out**.
- Wait 30 seconds, then log back in with your usual username and password.

**Step 3 — Restart your computer.**
If logging off did not help:
- Click **Start** \u2192 click the **Power** icon \u2192 select **Restart** (not Shut down).
- Wait for the computer to fully restart, then try logging in again.
- Allow up to **five minutes** for the login screen to appear — this is normal while the computer reconnects to the office system.

**Step 4 — If the problem continues after a restart, stop here and contact the Service Desk.**
Do not try to fix anything else yourself. The team will take it from here.

---

## Contact the Service Desk If\u2026

- You still cannot log in after restarting.
- You see an error message on screen when logging in.
- Other people near you (same office area or floor) have the same problem.
- Your programmes (email, shared drives or team files, Teams) are not loading after login.

---

## Information to Have Ready When You Call

Having these details ready will help the team fix the issue faster — you do not need to know what they mean, just read them out:

- [ ] Your **full name** and **job title**
- [ ] The **computer name** — there may be a sticker on your machine, or click the **Start** button, open **Settings** (the cog icon), click **System**, then click **About**. Look for the label **Device name** and read out what it says.
- [ ] The **floor and room** you are working in
- [ ] **What time** the problem started
- [ ] Whether **anyone else nearby** has the same issue
- [ ] The **exact wording** of any error message on screen (take a photo on your phone if it helps)

---

*If you are unsure about any step above, skip it and call the Service Desk straight away. There is no wrong answer \u2014 the team is here to help.*
