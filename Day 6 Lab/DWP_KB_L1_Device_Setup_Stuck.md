# My New Work Device Is Stuck and Will Not Finish Getting Ready

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 07/08/2026
**Status:** Draft

---

## Your data is safe

If your new device has stopped during its first-time preparation, please do not worry. Nothing has been lost. Your files, emails, and account are safely stored on our work systems, not on the device itself, and have not been affected in any way. This is a technical issue with the device, not with your account.

---

## What is happening?

When a new work device is handed to you, it goes through a series of getting-ready steps the first time you switch it on. Occasionally this can get stuck or show a warning on screen. This is a known issue that the IT team can fix quickly.

---

## What you can do right now

**Step 1.** Look at the screen and note down exactly what it says — including any numbers or codes shown in the warning. Take a photo with your phone if that is easier.

**Step 2.** Find the name printed on the label on your device (usually on the underside of a laptop or the back of a desktop). It will look something like `DESKTOP-AB123`. You do not need to type it — just read it out or send a photo when you contact the service desk.

**Step 3.** Do not turn the device off and on again repeatedly. Leave it on the screen it is stuck on.

**Step 4.** Do not try to sign in to any personal email or personal online accounts (such as a personal Microsoft or Google account) on this device.

**Step 5.** Contact the IT Service Desk using the details at the bottom of this article.

---

## Contact the IT Service Desk if…

- Your device shows a warning or message on the first screen shown when it was switched on
- The loading bar or moving dots on screen have not changed for more than 10 minutes
- The device turns itself off and on again more than once and returns to the same screen
- You are not sure whether the device has finished setting up properly

---

## What to tell the service desk

Have the following ready when you call or report the problem — this helps the team sort it out faster:

- [ ] Your full name and work email address
- [ ] The name on the label on your device (e.g. it will look like `DESKTOP-AB123` — just read it out)
- [ ] The date and time the issue started
- [ ] A description or photo of the message shown on screen
- [ ] Whether this is a brand new device or one that has been used before

The service desk will take it from there. You do not need to do anything else.
