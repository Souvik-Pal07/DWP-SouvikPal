# Black screen after logging in

If your work computer shows a black screen after you log in, please try these safe steps:

- Wait up to 30 seconds.
- If the screen is still black, choose Sign out.
- Log in again after 1 minute.
- If it still happens, restart your computer once and try again.
- If the black screen returns, stop trying and contact the help desk.

Your data is safe. This issue does not delete your files or remove your saved work.

Contact the help desk if the black screen happens more than once, you are disconnected after logging in, or you cannot get into your work computer after trying the steps above.

When you report the issue, please include:
- Your name
- Your account name
- The time the issue started
- Whether the screen goes black every time or only once
- Whether you can use other work devices normally
- A picture of the screen, if you can take one
