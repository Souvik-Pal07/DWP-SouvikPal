# DWP Service Desk Triage Summary

## Summary (one line)
Partner reports that Copilot surfaced and summarised a confidential draft settlement from a matter they are not assigned to.

## Impact (who/how many/business urgency)
- Who: Partner in legal team who was not assigned to the matter in question.
- How many: At minimum one unintended recipient; scope of broader oversharing unknown (to-verify).
- Business urgency: HIGH — potential data governance, confidentiality, and regulatory compliance breach; legal matter files exposed to unauthorised staff (to-verify).

## Known facts
- Ticket reference: T-1003.
- User group/context: Legal – Partner.
- Reported issue: Copilot surfaced a draft settlement document from a matter the partner is not assigned to and has no expectation of accessing.
- Copilot only surfaces content the user already has underlying SharePoint/OneDrive permissions to access; if Copilot showed this content, the user likely has over-broad permissions.
- Root cause is almost certainly a SharePoint permissions misconfiguration (e.g., broad site-member group, broken inheritance, or an overly permissive sharing link).

## Missing information to gather
- Whether the partner can manually navigate to and open the draft settlement file in SharePoint (to-verify).
- What SharePoint site/library/folder the draft settlement resides in and what permissions are assigned (to-verify).
- Whether the matter folder has unique permissions or inherits from a parent site (to-verify).
- Whether any organisation-wide or broad sharing links (e.g., "Anyone in the organisation") are applied to the file or folder (to-verify).
- Whether sensitivity labels or IRM/MIP protection are applied to the document (to-verify).
- How many other users may have inadvertent access to the same matter folder (to-verify).
- Whether a Data Protection/Information Governance team needs to be notified per firm policy (to-verify).
- Exact Copilot prompt and entry point used (to-verify).

## Likely category
SharePoint oversharing / permissions misconfiguration causing Copilot to surface confidential content to an unauthorised user — potential data governance incident (to-verify).

## First diagnostic step
Immediately escalate to the SharePoint administrator to review permissions on the matter folder/document and remove any overly broad access; simultaneously notify the Information Governance or Data Protection team per firm policy, as this may constitute an internal data exposure incident requiring logging.
