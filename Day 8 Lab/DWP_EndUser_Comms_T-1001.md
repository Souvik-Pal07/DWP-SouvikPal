## Copilot cannot access a document in SharePoint

Hi,

We have received your report that Microsoft Copilot returned "I don't have access to that content" when you asked it to summarise a client NDA stored in SharePoint. We wanted to explain what is happening and what you can do next.

**What is happening:**
Microsoft Copilot can only read documents that you already have permission to open yourself. If you heard about a folder in a meeting but have never actually opened it, you most likely do not have access to it yet — and Copilot will not be able to read it on your behalf. This is by design; Copilot respects your existing permissions and will never show you content you are not authorised to see.

**What to do next:**
1. Try opening the SharePoint folder and the NDA file directly in your browser.
2. If you see an "Access Denied" message or cannot find the file, this confirms you do not yet have the necessary permission.
3. Contact the Service Desk or the owner of that SharePoint site and ask them to grant you read access to the folder.
4. Once access is confirmed and you can open the file yourself, try asking Copilot to summarise it again — it should work at that point.

If you are unsure who owns the SharePoint site, please contact the Service Desk and we can help identify the right person to request access from.

**Contact the Service Desk:**
Raise a ticket via the self-service portal or call the helpdesk directly. Please quote reference **T-1001** in all correspondence.
