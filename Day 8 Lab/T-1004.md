- Business urgency: HIGH — full team productivity impact; all AI-assisted workflows blocked across the department (to-verify).

Bulk Microsoft 365 Copilot licence removal or group-based licence assignment change — possibly caused by an admin action or automated policy (to-verify).

Check the M365 admin centre (admin.microsoft.com > Billing > Licences > Microsoft 365 Copilot) to confirm whether Copilot licences are still assigned to Legal team members; simultaneously review the M365 admin centre Service Health dashboard for any active Copilot outage alerts; if licences are missing from the group, review recent group membership and licence assignment change history in Entra ID audit logs.
