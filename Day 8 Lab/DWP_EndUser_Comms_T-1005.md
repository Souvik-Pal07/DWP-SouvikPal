## Copilot is giving generic answers instead of reading your contract templates

Hi,

We have received your report that Copilot is giving vague, generic answers when you ask it about clauses in the contract templates library. We wanted to explain what is likely happening and share some steps that should improve your results straight away.

**What is happening:**
When Copilot gives generic answers rather than quoting specific content from your documents, it usually means it has not been able to read the actual files. This can happen for a few reasons:

- Copilot is answering from general knowledge rather than your specific documents.
- The files in the library may not yet be fully indexed by Microsoft 365.
- The documents may be scanned PDFs (images of text rather than typed text), which Copilot cannot read.

**What to do next:**
1. **Try opening the document first, then ask Copilot.** Open the specific contract template file directly in SharePoint or Word, then use the Copilot pane within that document to ask your clause question. This gives Copilot direct access to the file's content and tends to produce much better results.

2. **Be specific in your Copilot prompt.** When using Copilot in chat, paste the link to the document into your question. For example:
   > *"Summarise the indemnity clause in [paste SharePoint link here]."*

3. **Check the file format.** If the templates are scanned PDFs — that is, they look like photographs or photocopies of documents rather than typed text — Copilot cannot read them. Please let the Service Desk know if this is the case and we will explore whether the files can be converted to a readable format.

4. **If none of the above helps**, please contact the Service Desk. We will check whether the templates library has been fully indexed and whether your permissions are correctly configured.

**Contact the Service Desk:**
Raise a ticket via the self-service portal or call the helpdesk directly. Please quote reference **T-1005** in all correspondence.
