## Copilot in Outlook cannot find case emails

Hi,

We have received your report that Copilot in Outlook cannot find the case-related emails you need for context. We wanted to explain what is happening and what you can do next.

**What is happening:**
Copilot in Outlook can only work with emails that are already in your mailbox. As a new starter, there are two likely reasons why Copilot is not finding what you need:

- Your mailbox may still be in the process of being indexed by Microsoft 365. This is normal for new accounts and can take up to 72 hours to complete.
- The case emails you need may not have been forwarded or shared to your mailbox yet, meaning they simply are not there for Copilot to find.

**What to do next:**
1. First, check whether the emails exist in your Outlook inbox by searching manually — press **Ctrl+E** in Outlook and type a relevant keyword or case name.
2. If the emails are not in your mailbox, ask a colleague or your manager to forward the relevant case emails to you.
3. If the emails are in your mailbox but Copilot still cannot find them, this is most likely a short-term indexing delay. Please wait **24–48 hours** and try again.
4. If the issue continues after 48 hours, please contact the Service Desk and we will investigate further.

**Contact the Service Desk:**
Raise a ticket via the self-service portal or call the helpdesk directly. Please quote reference **T-1002** in all correspondence.
