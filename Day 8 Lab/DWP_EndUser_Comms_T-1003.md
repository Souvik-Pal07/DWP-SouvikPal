## Copilot showed a confidential document you should not have access to

Hi,

Thank you for reporting this to us straight away. We want to reassure you that we are treating this as a high-priority issue and are already taking action.

**What is happening:**
Microsoft Copilot only surfaces documents that the signed-in user already has permission to access in SharePoint. The fact that Copilot showed you a draft settlement from a matter you are not assigned to means that your account currently has access to that file — which should not be the case.

This is a permissions configuration issue, not a fault with your account. It has been escalated immediately to the SharePoint administrator and the Information Governance team to investigate and correct.

**What to do next:**
1. **Please do not open, save, share, print, or act on the document you were shown.**
2. You do not need to investigate or do anything further — the Service Desk has already escalated this on your behalf.
3. The SharePoint administrator will review and correct the permissions on the matter folder as a priority.
4. We will contact you once the issue has been resolved and the permissions have been confirmed as correct.
5. If you have any concerns about confidentiality or data handling in the meantime, please speak directly to your Information Governance or Data Protection contact.

**Contact the Service Desk:**
Raise a ticket via the self-service portal or call the helpdesk directly. Please quote reference **T-1003** in all correspondence.
